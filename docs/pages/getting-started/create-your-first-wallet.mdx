# Create Your First Wallet

Set up your wallet in seconds — just ask your AI assistant.

## Create a new wallet

**1.** Tell Claude:

> "Create a new Keplr wallet called my-wallet"

**2.** Claude will generate a wallet and store it securely in your OS credential store (e.g. macOS Keychain). You'll see your wallet addresses for Cosmos chains.

**3.** Verify it worked:

> "Show my accounts"

Your new wallet should appear as the active account.

**That's it!** Your wallet is ready to use. The recovery phrase is encrypted and stored locally — it is not displayed in the conversation.

## Import an existing wallet

**1.** Tell Claude:

> "Import my wallet called trading with mnemonic: word1 word2 ... word24"

:::warning
This will leave your mnemonic as plain text in the conversation history. To avoid exposure, use the `KEPLR_MNEMONIC` environment variable instead. See [Environment Variables](/reference/environment#keplr_mnemonic) for details.
:::

**2.** Claude will import the wallet and store it securely in your OS credential store (e.g. macOS Keychain). You'll see your wallet addresses for Cosmos chains.

**3.** Verify it worked:

> "Show my accounts"

Your imported wallet should appear as the active account.

## What can I do next?

Once your wallet is set up, try asking Claude:

- **"Show my balances on Cosmos Hub"** — Check your token balances
- **"Stake 100 ATOM to a good validator"** — Start earning rewards
- **"Swap 50 OSMO for ATOM"** — Trade tokens on Osmosis

## Export your recovery phrase

If you need to back up your recovery phrase:

> "Export my recovery phrase"

**Keep it safe.** The 24-word recovery phrase is the only way to restore your wallet. Write it down and store it offline — never share it with anyone.
