# Check Balances

View your token balances across chains.

## How to Check

```
You: "Show my balance on Cosmos Hub"
You: "Show my balances on Osmosis and Celestia"
You: "Show all my balances"
You: "Show my portfolio"
```

You can also check your total balance including staked tokens:

```
You: "Show my total ATOM including staked"
```
