# Governance

Vote on on-chain proposals.

## How to Vote

```
You: "Show active proposals on Cosmos Hub"
You: "Vote yes on proposal 123"
You: "Vote no with veto on proposal 456"
```

You can vote **Yes**, **No**, **Abstain**, or **No With Veto**.

Your voting power is based on how much you've staked. If you don't vote, your validator votes on your behalf.
