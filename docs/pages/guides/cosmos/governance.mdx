# Governance

Vote on on-chain proposals.

## View proposals

```
You: "Show active proposals on Cosmos Hub"
You: "Show me proposal 123 on Cosmos Hub"
```

When viewing a specific proposal, the AI shows the title, description, current tally, and voting deadline so you can make an informed decision.

## Vote

```
You: "Vote yes on proposal 123"
You: "Vote no with veto on proposal 456 on Osmosis"
```

You can vote **Yes**, **No**, **Abstain**, or **No With Veto**. The AI previews your vote and the fee before you confirm.

Your voting power equals the amount you have staked on that chain. If you haven't voted by the deadline, your validator votes on your behalf.

:::tip
Use the `/governance` slash command for a guided flow — it shows active proposals, lets you read the details, and walks you through voting.
:::

## Tips

- **Vote before the deadline** — The AI shows the voting end date. Once it passes, your vote can't be submitted.
- **Specify the chain** — If you're active on multiple chains, include the chain name: `"Vote yes on proposal 123 on Cosmos Hub"`.
