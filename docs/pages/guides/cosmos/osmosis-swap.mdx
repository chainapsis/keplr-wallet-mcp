# Osmosis Swap

Swap tokens on Osmosis DEX.

## How to swap

```
You: "Swap 100 OSMO for ATOM"
You: "Swap 50 ATOM for USDC on Osmosis"
```

### What to expect

The AI shows you:
- Exchange rate and the amount you'll receive
- Swap fee and price impact
- Minimum received (accounting for slippage)

Nothing executes until you confirm.

## Get a quote first

For larger trades, check the rate before committing:

```
You: "How much ATOM would I get for 1000 OSMO?"
You: "Quote swapping 500 USDC for OSMO"
```

Quotes show the exchange rate and price impact without creating a transaction. This is useful for checking whether the current rate is favorable or if price impact is too high on a large order.

## Slippage

You can set a custom slippage tolerance:

```
You: "Swap 100 OSMO for ATOM with 2% slippage"
```

If the price moves beyond your slippage tolerance between the quote and execution, the swap will be rejected to protect you.

## Tips

- **Check price impact on large swaps** — A quote will show you the price impact percentage. High impact means you're moving the price against yourself.
- **Tokens must be on Osmosis** — Your tokens need to be on the Osmosis chain. If they're on another chain, [IBC transfer](/guides/cosmos/send#cross-chain-transfer-ibc) them to Osmosis first.
