# Send Tokens

Transfer tokens on Cosmos chains.

## How to send

```
You: "Send 10 ATOM to cosmos1abc..."
You: "Send 100 OSMO to osmo1abc... on Osmosis"
```

### What to expect

1. The AI shows a **preview** — amount, recipient, estimated fee. Nothing moves yet.
2. You **review and confirm** — say "yes" to proceed or "no" to cancel.
3. The AI executes the transaction and shows you the result with a transaction hash.

The confirmation token expires after 5 minutes. If it expires, just ask again.

## Cross-chain transfer (IBC)

To send tokens to a different chain, just describe what you want — the AI uses IBC automatically:

```
You: "Send 50 ATOM from Cosmos Hub to Osmosis"
You: "Send 50 ATOM from Cosmos Hub to osmo1abc... on Osmosis"
You: "Send my ATOM from Osmosis back to Cosmos Hub"
```

### What to expect

- The AI finds the right IBC channel automatically (this may take a moment the first time).
- IBC transfers typically complete within 30 seconds to a few minutes.
- Tokens arrive on the destination chain as IBC-wrapped denominations, but the AI displays friendly names.

## Tips

- **Leave enough for fees** — Don't send your entire balance. Keep a small amount for the transaction fee.
- **Double-check addresses** — Each chain has its own prefix (`cosmos1...` for Cosmos Hub, `osmo1...` for Osmosis, `celestia1...` for Celestia). The AI will warn you if the prefix doesn't match the chain.

## Common issues

- **"Insufficient funds"** — Your balance is too low for the amount + fee. Try a smaller amount.
- **"Invalid address"** — Check that the address prefix matches the chain.
- **"Account does not exist"** — Your account needs to receive tokens at least once before it can send. Fund it first.
- **IBC transfer stuck** — IBC transfers occasionally time out on congested channels. Check your balance on both chains — the tokens are never lost, they'll either arrive or be refunded.
