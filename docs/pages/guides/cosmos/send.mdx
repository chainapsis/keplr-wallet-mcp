# Send Tokens

Transfer tokens on Cosmos chains.

## How to Send

```
You: "Send 10 ATOM to cosmos1abc..."
You: "Send 100 OSMO to osmo1abc... on Osmosis"
```

The AI will show you a preview (amount, recipient, fee) before executing. Nothing happens until you confirm.

## Memo

```
You: "Send 10 ATOM to cosmos1abc... with memo 12345"
```

When sending to an exchange, a memo is usually required — check the exchange's deposit instructions.

:::warning
Never put your recovery phrase or sensitive information in a memo. Memos are publicly visible on the blockchain.
:::

## Cross-Chain Transfer

To send tokens to a different chain, just ask — the AI will automatically use IBC:

```
You: "Send 50 ATOM from Cosmos Hub to Osmosis"
You: "Send 50 ATOM from Cosmos Hub to osmo1abc... on Osmosis"
You: "Send my ATOM from Osmosis back to Cosmos Hub"
```

Some routes go through intermediate chains. The AI handles this automatically:

```
You: "Send USDC from Noble to Cosmos Hub"
```

- **Transfer taking long** — The AI may take some time to find the right channel. Once the transaction is submitted, it usually completes quickly.
- **Tokens not appearing** — Check the destination chain balance. IBC tokens may show as a different denom — the AI will display the friendly name when possible.

## Common Issues

- **"Insufficient funds"** — Your balance is too low for the amount + fee. Try a smaller amount.
- **"Invalid address"** — Check that the address prefix matches the chain (e.g. `cosmos1...` for Cosmos Hub, `osmo1...` for Osmosis).
- **"Account does not exist"** — Your account needs to receive tokens at least once before it can send. Fund it first.
